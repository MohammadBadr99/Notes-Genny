# Notes-Geny
This tool automates the on-site (Timeline and SSR WO) notes. 

It crawls the MSD data to get Personal details of customer and his device.

It takes the serial number of the device and goes to the Lenovo website then pastes the serial number and searches for the parts and brings them into our interface

After showing the parts in the interface, we can filter on the desired part by search or drop down menu.

Finally after selecting all the parts needed, we export the data to get the final notes with Customer details and the parts that we are desiring to dispatch.
